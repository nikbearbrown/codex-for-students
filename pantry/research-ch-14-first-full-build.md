## 6. Pedagogical Delivery Research

### Prior knowledge required
Everything.

### Common misconceptions to disarm
- **"Framework is too elaborate for a small build."** Scales.
- **"I'll skip the framework on easy parts."** Easy parts are where dangerous middle lives.
- **"Post-build doc is for the framework, not for me."** No. For future-self.
- **"I'm not ready."** Practice, not readiness, is the threshold.

### Effective instructional sequences
- **Project brief in detail.** Concrete.
- **Student's hand on every decision.** Resist over-helping.
- **Closing paragraph.** TIKTOC's supplied closing.

### Known failure modes
- **The chapter as homework.** Frame as a gift — a small useful tool.
- **The "your build was wrong" experience.** Pre-equip with normalization.
- **The book's voice continuing too long.** Don't over-stay.

### What separates understanding from memorization
A reader who *understands* Ch 14 produces a post-build document containing a specific insight they didn't have at the start of the book. A reader who memorized Ch 14 produces a generic document.
